Home / FAQ

Fence questions, straight answers.

Everything Toledo homeowners ask us before they build — costs, permits, property lines, warranties and winter installs. If your question isn't here, call and the owner will answer it himself.

How much does a fence cost in Toledo?

Fence pricing depends on the style, height, length, gates and site requirements. In our ballpark tool, enter an approximate length or sketch your planned fence on the map, then choose your style, height and gates. Share your contact details to view the planning range and request a project review. Some selections need team review before pricing. The range is not a fixed-price quote; a site measure and written proposal confirm the scope and price.

How long does a fence installation take?

Most residential fences are installed in 1–3 days once materials arrive. From your first call, you'll typically have your written estimate within 24–48 hours, and most projects are scheduled within a few weeks depending on the season. The owner supervises every install, so the crew that starts your fence finishes it.

Do I need a permit for a fence in Toledo or the surrounding areas?

Usually, yes — and the rules differ by municipality. Toledo, Sylvania, Perrysburg, Maumee and the Michigan townships each have their own height limits and setback rules, and front yards are almost always limited to lower heights than back yards. We handle the permit process for you as part of every job, so you never have to stand in line at a zoning office.

Who marks the underground utilities?

We do — before any post hole is dug, we call in the dig tickets so buried gas, electric, water and communication lines are marked. It's the law in both Ohio and Michigan, and it's included in every installation. Never let anyone dig on your property without it.

What if my property line isn't clear?

Use the map to sketch where you would like the fence to go. Parcel overlays, where available, are approximate planning references and do not establish a legal property boundary. If the boundary is unclear, resolve its location before installation; a licensed surveyor can establish it. You can still start your ballpark request with an approximate fence length while the layout is being confirmed.

Does every job include a workmanship warranty?

Every job includes a 3-year workmanship warranty from Ghareeb Fencing Solutions. Ask us for the written workmanship warranty and the material warranty information for the products in your project.

Do you offer financing?

Explore financing options through Slice by FNBO. Approval, APR and repayment terms depend on the offer for which you qualify. Review the lender’s current terms before accepting financing. You can request a fence quote first.

Review financing information

Can you install a fence in winter?

In most cases, yes. Northwest Ohio frost doesn't stop a properly equipped crew — we dig below the frost line year-round. Winter is often the fastest time to get on the schedule, and your fence is ready the moment spring arrives.

Will you remove my old fence?

Yes. Tear-out and haul-away of your existing fence is a standard option on every quote — you'll see it as a line item so the price is transparent, never a surprise add-on.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Toledo and build throughout Northwest Ohio and Southeast Michigan — Sylvania, Perrysburg, Maumee, Rossford, Northwood, Bowling Green, and across the state line to Monroe, Temperance, Lambertville, Whiteford and Dundee. If you're within about an hour of Toledo, you're local to us.
