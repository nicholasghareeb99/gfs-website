Cedar

Privacy
Depends on the board pattern; lattice and shadowbox leave visible openings.
Upkeep
Choose natural weathering or a finish with ongoing care.
Open views
Spaced pickets or a decorative top leave openings where a view matters.
Gates and access
Include gate framing, swing and usable opening in the design.
Planning your layout
Review whether the top follows the slope or steps between sections.
Explore cedar fencing →

Treated wood

Privacy
Butted boards can develop gaps as wet lumber dries; overlap changes the sightline.
Upkeep
Inspect wood and hardware; plan coating care if a finished color matters.
Open views
Shadowbox allows angled views; overlapping boards provide different screening.
Gates and access
Include gate framing and hardware along with the boards.
Planning your layout
Discuss sloped runs and the bottom gap before fixing the board layout.
Explore treated wood fencing →

Vinyl

Privacy
Solid infill screens views; picket and decorative openings do not.
Upkeep
Routine washing and hardware checks; follow the product's care instructions.
Open views
Picket and decorative openings keep views; solid infill screens them.
Gates and access
Gate-post reinforcement and hardware depend on the selected system.
Planning your layout
Some systems rack; others need a different layout. Confirm the actual limit.
Explore vinyl fencing →

Ornamental aluminum

Privacy
Open pickets preserve sightlines rather than providing a solid screen.
Upkeep
Clean the coating gently and inspect gates and damaged areas.
Open views
Open pickets define the yard while keeping the landscape visible.
Gates and access
Review latch position, opening and swing independently of the panel style.
Planning your layout
Rackability varies by system; bottom gaps still need review.
Explore ornamental aluminum fencing →

Chain link

Privacy
Open mesh; slats change screening and require a system review.
Upkeep
Inspect coating, ties, tension and gate operation.
Open views
Open mesh keeps the yard visible. Discuss future screening before choosing supports.
Gates and access
Specify walking and equipment gates separately.
Planning your layout
Review the bottom line at dips, corners and transitions.
Explore chain link fencing →

Split rail

Privacy
Open rails leave the landscape visible.
Upkeep
Wood care depends on species, treatment and chosen finish.
Open views
Rails keep the landscape open; wire backing is a separate enclosure choice.
Gates and access
Plan entrances with the correct end and gate-post arrangement.
Planning your layout
Check how rails meet at grade changes; wire backing needs its own bottom detail.
Explore split rail fencing →

The layout and exact product matter as much as the material. Review ground gaps, gate details and the selected product’s care instructions with our team. Pet enclosures and pool barriers need their own design review.
