What holds the fence up

The part you never see fails first. We pick the post by your soil, and we check it at the estimate.

Clay · most Toledo yards

Driven steel post (no-dig)

  • 8 ft galvanized steel post, 2⅜″ across, driven 4 ft down, below the frost line
  • No hole and no concrete, so no dirt piles and nothing to heave in a freeze
  • No cure time: the fence goes on as soon as the posts are in
  • We can drive posts in frozen ground, so we build all winter
Sand or loose soil

Post set in concrete

  • Driven posts need clay to grip, so sandy yards get dug posts
  • Pressure-treated wood post in a 10″ hole, 36″ deep, set in concrete
  • Galvanized steel in concrete is available as an upgrade
  • Gate posts get a wider 12″ hole

Split rail is the exception: it uses mortised cedar or pressure-treated posts. More on no-dig fencing.
