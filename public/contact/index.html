FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a free estimate?

You can get a free estimate by calling us at (419) 214-2107, filling out our contact form above, or using our instant ballpark quote tool for a structured project profile that helps us prepare a faster, more accurate on-site visit.

What areas do you serve?

We serve all of Northwest Ohio and Southeast Michigan, including Toledo, Perrysburg, Maumee, Sylvania, Bowling Green, Monroe, and many more communities. Check our service areas page for the full list.

How quickly can you start my project?

During peak season (April–October), our typical lead time is 1-3 weeks from approved contract to installation. We can often accommodate urgent projects — call us to discuss your timeline.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. We research permit requirements for your municipality and can assist with the application process. We also call OUPS to get utility lines marked before any digging starts.

Do you offer financing?

Yes — qualified homeowners can access 0% APR financing through First National Bank of Omaha (Slice). Apply in 60 seconds with no impact on your credit score. Ask us for the link.
