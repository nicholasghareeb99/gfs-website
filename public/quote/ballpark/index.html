How to sketch your fence
  1. Find your property. Enter the full address, select it and zoom in. Move map lets you position the view.
  2. Tap your fence route. Choose Draw fence. Tap the start, every corner and the end. You do not need to trace the whole property.
  3. Finish the line. Tap Finish line. Length fills in automatically. Use Undo for a mistake, or Draw fence for another section.

Example sketch only. After drawing, use Add gates for openings. You can also skip the map and enter a length.
