A plan for your property

Separate replacement work from layout changes

Replacing the same run and moving a fence to a new location are different project discussions. Mark the existing line, the proposed line and any gates you want to relocate so the estimate covers the actual change.

Look at how the chosen style will meet the house, garage and remaining fence. Consistent transitions and a useful gate location often make as much difference as the panel design.

Bring these details to your estimate

  • Draw existing and proposed fence lines in different colors.
  • List gates to retain, replace or move.
  • Photograph the connections to the house and remaining fence sections.

LOCAL FENCE PLANNING

Fence permits in Southgate

Southgate lists fences among residential projects that usually require a permit; confirm your scope.

What to know before installation

  • The Building Department lists a fence permit form and a fence agreement in lieu of survey.
  • It separately lists a form for homeowners replacing a fence in its existing location.
  • Applications requiring plan review must be made in person according to the department page.
What to prepare

Use this checklist for your conversation with the local office.

  • Recommended: existing and proposed fence layouts, height, material and gate changes.
  • Recommended: available survey and identification of sections to replace in place; ask which fence forms apply.
  • Required if the homeowner performs the work: the department says a homeowner affidavit must be filed.

More questions about your fence layout

Is replacing a Southgate fence the same as moving it?

They can involve different scope and placement questions. Show both locations on the plan and check the current city process before assuming that an existing line or approval applies to a relocated fence.

Does a site plan check replace a boundary survey?

Our site-plan review helps organize the proposed fence layout and flag property-line questions. It is a planning reference, not a boundary survey or a determination of property ownership. Uncertain boundaries need to be resolved before installation.
