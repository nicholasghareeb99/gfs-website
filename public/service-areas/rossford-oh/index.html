Local Knowledge

Make the connections part of the plan

A new fence should have a clear beginning and end. Photograph the points where it may meet the house, a garage or an existing fence, and identify who owns each remaining section. Include transitions between materials and any changes in ground level so the quoted scope reflects the whole enclosure.

LOCAL FENCE PLANNING

Fence permits in Rossford

An approved fence permit is required for every fence.

What to know before installation

  • Section 1134.04 requires a permit regardless of fence height or type.
  • The application requests a site plan, fence type and height, and distance from the property line. Connecting to an existing fence requires its owner’s written permission under the code.
What to prepare

Use this checklist for your conversation with the local office.

  • Recommended: property address and a sketch showing the fence, gates, structures and property lines.
  • Recommended: fence height and material, available survey and any association or easement documents.
