Meet the Owner

A local who shows up on every job.

Most fence companies hand you off the moment you sign the contract. We don't. Here's why.

The Ghareeb story.

Nick Ghareeb founded Ghareeb Fencing Solutions in 2022 after years in landscaping and outdoor construction. The decision came after watching too many neighbors get burned by big national chains — quoted by a salesperson, installed by a sub-crew they'd never meet, then ghosted when something went wrong.

Nick built the company around a single operating rule: the person who sells you the project is the same person who installs it and stands behind it. No phone tree. No subcontractors. No handoffs.

Every job includes our 3-year workmanship warranty. We put your options, scope and warranty terms in writing, with material coverage explained separately for the products in your proposal.

"If I wouldn't install it on my own mother's property, we don't install it on yours. It's that simple."
Nick Ghareeb Owner · Ghareeb Fencing Solutions
